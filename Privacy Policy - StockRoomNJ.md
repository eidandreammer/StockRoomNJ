# **Privacy Policy**

**Effective Date:** 6/19/2026

Welcome to The Stock Room NJ, operated by Bitesites LLC ("we," "us," or "our"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website, stockroomnj.com, and use our platform for bidding, purchasing, or guest checkouts (collectively, the "Platform").

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this privacy policy, please do not access the site.

### **1\. Information We Collect**

We may collect information about you in a variety of ways when you interact with our Platform.

**A. Personal Data You Provide to Us**

* **Account Registration:** If you create an account, we collect your name, email address, phone number, shipping and billing addresses, and age verification data.  
* **Guest Checkout:** If you make a purchase without creating an account, we still collect necessary information to fulfill your order, including your name, email, phone number, shipping/billing addresses, and age verification data.  
* **Financial Data:** Payment information (such as credit card numbers) is required to process transactions and secure bids. *Note: We do not store full credit card numbers on our servers; this data is securely processed and managed by our third-party payment processors (e.g., Stripe, PayPal).*  
* **Transaction & Bidding History:** We maintain records of the items you bid on, win, and purchase, both as an account holder and a guest.

**B. Automatically Collected Data**

When you access the Platform, our servers automatically record information that your browser sends. This may include:

* Your IP address.  
* Browser type and version.  
* Operating system.  
* Your activity on the Platform (pages viewed, time spent, links clicked).  
* Device information (e.g., mobile device ID if accessing via a smartphone).

**C. Cookies and Tracking Technologies**

We use cookies, web beacons, and similar tracking technologies to customize the Platform, improve your experience, and remember your login session or shopping cart contents.

### **2\. How We Use Your Information**

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

* **Fulfillment & Operations:** To process your bids, facilitate direct purchases, fulfill orders, process refunds, and deliver items to you.  
* **Account Management:** To create and manage your account, or facilitate seamless guest checkouts.  
* **Communication:** To send you transactional emails (order confirmations, shipping updates, outbid notices) and respond to your customer service inquiries.  
* **Platform Security:** To verify age and eligibility, prevent fraudulent transactions, monitor against automated bots, and enforce our Terms of Service.  
* **Marketing:** To send you newsletters, promotions, and updates about new inventory (you may opt out of marketing emails at any time).  
* **Improvement:** To analyze website usage and improve our platform's functionality and user experience.

### **3\. How We Share Your Information**

We do not sell your personal data. We may share your information with third parties only in the following circumstances:

* **Service Providers:** We share data with trusted third-party vendors who assist us in operating our platform, such as payment processors, shipping and fulfillment partners, email service providers, and fraud-prevention services.  
* **Legal Obligations:** We may disclose your information if required to do so by law, subpoena, or court order, or to protect the rights, property, and safety of BiteSites LLC, our users, or others.  
* **Business Transfers:** In the event of a merger, sale of company assets, financing, or acquisition of all or a portion of our business, your information may be transferred to the new owners.

### **4\. Data Security**

We implement commercially reasonable administrative, technical, and physical security measures to help protect your personal information from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure. However, no electronic transmission over the internet or information storage technology can be guaranteed to be 100% secure, so we cannot promise or guarantee that hackers, cybercriminals, or other unauthorized third parties will not be able to defeat our security and improperly collect, access, steal, or modify your information.

### **5\. Your Rights and Choices**

* **Account Information:** You may review, update, or delete the information in your account by logging into your account settings or contacting us directly.  
* **Emails and Communications:** You may opt out of receiving promotional emails by using the "unsubscribe" link included in those emails. Transactional emails related to your orders or bids cannot be opted out of.  
* **Cookies:** Most web browsers are set to accept cookies by default. You can usually choose to set your browser to remove or reject cookies, though this may affect certain features of the Platform.

### **6\. Children's Privacy**

Our Platform is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 18\. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 18\. If we become aware that we have collected personal data from a child under 18 without verification of parental consent, we will take steps to remove that information from our servers.

### **7\. Changes to This Privacy Policy**

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or relevant laws. We will notify you of any material changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on the Platform and updating the "Effective Date." We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
